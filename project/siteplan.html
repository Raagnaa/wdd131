Site Name

Dreamscapes to visit – This name reflects the purpose of the site: showcasing beautiful landscapes and recommended destinations to visit.

Site Purpose

The site will serve as a visual and practical guide for travelers and nature lovers. It will include descriptions, photos, travel tips, and a gallery organized by categories.

Scenarios

  • What is the best season to visit popular beaches?
  • What activities can I do in a forest during autumn?
  • Where can I find practical information to plan my trip?

Color Scheme

  • Deep Green (#1B4D3E) – Used for headings and accents, reinforcing the natural tones in the logo.
  • Ocean Blue (#3A6EA5) – Used for backgrounds and links, complementing cooler tones in the logo.
  • Warm Beige (#E6D5B8) – Used for tips and detail sections, balancing with the earthy warmth of the logo.

Typography

  • Montserrat – Titles and headings.
  • Roboto – Paragraphs and main content.
  • Lora – Quotes or highlighted text.
