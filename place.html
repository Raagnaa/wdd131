Welcome to the National Park Banff

Rocky Mountain peaks, glacial lakes, and adventure come together in Banff National Park - Canada’s first national park and the flagship of the nation’s park system. Banff is part of the Canadian Rocky Mountain Parks UNESCO World Heritage Site.

About the place

The dense forests, glacial waters, soaring peaks, and plunging canyons of Banff National Park provide important habitat for 56 species of mammals, 300 species of birds, and more than 800 species of plants. Building on a long history of wildlife research and conservation, Parks Canada continues to work to protect and restore this special place for future generations.

Practical Information

Banff National Park is open year-round, located 150 km west of Calgary. Admission requires a Parks Canada pass, with extra fees for camping and backcountry permits. Summer offers hiking and lake visits, while winter is ideal for skiing. Weather changes quickly—pack layers and be prepared for wildlife encounters. Book accommodations early, especially in peak season.
